Bus Sort Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Bus Sort ("the app") is a puzzle game published by Hakaru Games. The app is intended for players aged 13 and over.

What we collect

We (Hakaru Games) do not collect, store or sell personal information about you. The app has no user accounts, no chat and no analytics, and we do not run servers that receive your data. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described in Advertising.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (current level, coins, boosters, hints, tutorial progress and settings) is saved on your device so you can continue where you left off. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in your phone's Settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob: short full-screen ads between some levels, and optional video ads you can choose to watch for a reward (such as a free hint or an extra booster). To show, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud, Google AdMob may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID) and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (such as device model, operating system, language and app version) and how you interact with ads (for example impressions and taps).

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us, and is shared with Google for advertising purposes. Depending on your consent and settings, ads may be personalised based on your interests. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your ad choices and consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent using Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices. Wherever you are, you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's Settings (Google → Ads, or Security & privacy → Ads), and learn more at Google Ad Settings. If you opt out, you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant.

Deleting your data

To delete your Bus Sort data:

  • Game progress on your phone: uninstall the app, or clear its data in your phone's Settings (Apps → Bus Sort → Storage → Clear data).
  • Advertising data: we hold none. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings (Google → Ads). Data collected by Google AdMob is handled under the Google Privacy Policy, and you can manage it at myaccount.google.com.

We keep no copy of any of this data ourselves. If you need help, email info@hakaruinfotech.com.

Permissions

The app uses the internet and the advertising ID permission so Google AdMob can show ads. It does not access your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The only third-party service the app uses is Google AdMob, described above. If you believe a child under 13 is using the app, contact us; you can also reset the advertising ID in the phone's Settings.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to Hakaru Games at info@hakaruinfotech.com or via hakaruinfotech.com.
